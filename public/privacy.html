Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register for an account, we collect your username, email address, and a secure hash of your password. We also record your offline/online practice session statistics (WPM, accuracy, consistency, duration, error keys, and completion date) to construct your profile data.

2. Use of Data

Your session statistics are used to display global leaderboard rankings, compute personal records, calculate XP gains, and unlock level achievements. Your email is exclusively used to uniquely identify your session token and handle secure authentication logins.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your registration profile or credentials with third-party advertising brokers. Your public profile parameters (username, level, registration date, achievements, and top speed metrics) are displayed on the public leaderboards for active competition.

4. Cookies & Storage

We use browser local storage (LocalStorage) to store your system customizer configurations (font family, font size, active theme choice, layout structure, and session tokens). We do not deploy third-party advertising tracking cookies.

5. Data Deletion

Should you wish to close your account and permanently remove your typing records from our database, please contact support at contact@smashkeys.com, and we will process your deletion request.
